02 — ROLES

Earned, not given.

Three ladders. Self-selected interests, staff-reviewed credentials, and a clear line between them — a verified role means review, never endorsement.

Staff

Administrator · Moderator

Keeps the space safe and useful.

Verified

Verified Founder · Verified Investor · Verified Business Owner

Application + human review. Investors reviewed strictly by hand.

Members

Founder · Entrepreneur · Developer · Designer · Marketer · Investor Interest · Student Founder

Picked at onboarding — never a credential.

03 — PRINCIPLES

The house rules.

Short version — the full text lives in #rules.

  1. 01
    Respect the room. No harassment, hate, or discrimination. Professional people, direct conversations.
  2. 02
    No spam or shilling. No unsolicited ads, mass DMs, or self-promotion outside the channels built for it.
  3. 03
    No scams — ever. No guaranteed returns, pyramid schemes, fake credentials, or deceptive fundraising.
  4. 04
    Protect privacy. No doxxing, no sharing private information, no malicious links.
  5. 05
    Build honestly. Opportunities are labelled — paid, equity, volunteer, exploratory. No exploitation.
